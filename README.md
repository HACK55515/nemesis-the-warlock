# NEMESIS: THE WARLOCK

> **The Termight Empire wants to purge all alien life.** As Nemesis, fire-breathing warlock of Khaos, fight back—and burn their tyranny to ash.

A dark-fantasy arena action game that runs in your browser. Face waves of Termight forces, unleash fire and chaos, and see how long you can survive.

## Play

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a modern desktop or mobile browser, or serve the project locally:

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) and choose **BEGIN THE HUNT**. No build step or package installation is required. The game loads Three.js from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed when you play.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse |
| --- | --- |
| Move | **W** **A** **S** **D** or **Arrow keys** |
| Aim | **Move the mouse** over the arena |
| Fire a fireball | **Left-click** or **Space** |
| Sword slash | **F** |
| Chaos Blast (uses mana) | **Q** |
| Pause / resume | **P** |

### Mobile

Use the **left virtual joystick** to move and the **right-hand touch area** to fire. The on-screen controls appear on touch-oriented and smaller screens.

## How to play

- Move around the arena to avoid incoming enemies and attacks.
- Aim with your mouse and use fireballs to strike enemies at range.
- Use your sword when foes get close; unleash Chaos Blast when you need a powerful mana-consuming attack.
- Watch your **health** and **mana** in the HUD. Track your **wave**, **score**, and **kills** as the fight escalates.
- Survive the enemy waves and face the final threat. The hunt ends if your health runs out—try again and beat your previous score.

## Features

- 3D arena combat, powered by Three.js
- Keyboard, mouse, and touch controls
- Multiple attacks, enemy encounters, and escalating waves
- Health and mana HUD, score tracking, and end-game screens
- Dark, atmospheric visuals with lighting, particles, and synthesized sound effects
